About Me

Hey, I'm Tyler

I graduated from The University of British Columbia with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in December 2023.

I took a gap year after high school and lived in Kyoto, Japan for a year — just a short walk from Fushimi Inari, the most visited tourist spot in the world. It gave me the time and space to figure out who I was and what I actually wanted, and I'm a big supporter of others doing the same.

My main interests are computer graphics, game development, and game engine creation. My favorite games are Dark Souls, Persona 5, Skyrim, Portal 2, Undertale, and World of Warcraft Classic.
